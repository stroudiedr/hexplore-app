Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Hexplore is built on a simple principle: your data belongs to you. This policy explains what information Hexplore collects, how it is used, and what we never do with it.

What Hexplore collects

Hexplore stores the following data on your device:

  • Explored hex tile IDs — when you move through an area, your GPS position is converted into an anonymised hexagonal grid cell identifier (H3 cell ID). This cell ID is stored, not your raw GPS coordinates.
  • Activity metadata — date, duration, distance, transport mode, and source (live tracking, Strava, or GPX file) for each activity.
  • App preferences — settings such as your chosen transport mode filter, display preferences, and onboarding status.

What Hexplore never collects

  • Your raw GPS coordinates are never stored. Location data is converted to H3 cell IDs in memory and immediately discarded.
  • No personal information — no name, email address, or account is required to use Hexplore.
  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising identifiers.
  • No crash reporting or telemetry data is sent to any server.

Where your data is stored

All data collected by Hexplore is stored exclusively on your device. It is never uploaded to any server, never shared with third parties, and never leaves your device except as described in the third-party services section below.

Third-party services

Hexplore integrates with one optional third-party service:

Strava
If you choose to connect your Strava account, Hexplore uses the Strava API to read your activity history. Hexplore requests read-only access (activity:read_all) and never writes to or modifies your Strava account. Your Strava credentials are stored securely in the iOS Keychain on your device and are never transmitted to any Hexplore server. Strava's own privacy policy applies to data held by Strava: strava.com/legal/privacy

Map tiles (OpenStreetMap)
Hexplore displays maps using MapLibre Native, an open-source rendering library, with map tile data sourced from OpenStreetMap contributors. When you view the map, your device makes standard tile requests to retrieve map data. These requests include your approximate viewport location, which is inherent to any map-based application. No account, API key, or commercial SDK is involved in this process. OpenStreetMap's privacy policy applies to tile requests: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy

Location access

Hexplore requests access to your device's location to support live activity tracking. We recommend granting "Always Allow" so that tracking continues when your screen is locked during a run or walk. Location data is used only to convert your position into H3 cell IDs for map reveal — it is never stored as raw coordinates and never transmitted off your device.

You can withdraw location permission at any time in iOS Settings. Strava import and GPX import remain fully functional without location access.

In-app purchases

Hexplore Pro is available as a one-time purchase via Apple's App Store. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. Hexplore does not collect or store any payment information.

Children's privacy

Hexplore is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact
