Reveal Your World

Every run, walk, and ride you've ever taken has carved a path through the world. But how much of it have you actually seen?

Hexplore turns your movement into a living map. The world starts hidden beneath a fog of war — and only you can lift it. As you move, the fog burns away to reveal the map beneath, tile by tile, in a satisfying hexagonal grid.

How it works

Your exploration, beautifully mapped

Hexplore uses a hexagonal grid to track everywhere you've been. Each hex tile you reveal is permanently yours — a record of every street, trail, and path you've ever covered. Watch your personal map grow over time.

🏃

Built for runners, walkers & cyclists

Connect Strava to seed your map with your full history, import GPX files, or take your phone out and watch the fog lift in real time as you move.

⬡

Frontier highlighting

See exactly where your explored territory ends — perfect for planning new routes into unmapped areas and pushing your boundaries further.

🚴

Filter by transport mode

See what you've covered on foot versus by bike. Each mode has its own colour on the map so your exploration story is always clear.

📊

Track your stats

Total area explored, activities logged, personal bests, and more. Watch your numbers grow as you reveal more of the world around you.

🔒

Your data stays yours

All your exploration data is stored privately on your device. Nothing is shared, sold, or uploaded. Raw GPS coordinates are never stored — only anonymised hex tile IDs. Hexplore simply helps you see the world you've already moved through — and inspires you to go further.
