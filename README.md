# hexplore-app
Hexplore — Reveal Your World

Every run, walk, and ride you've ever taken has carved a path through the world. But how much of it have you actually seen?

Hexplore turns your movement into a living map. The world starts hidden beneath a fog of war — and only you can lift it. As you run, walk, or cycle through an area, the fog burns away to reveal the map beneath, tile by tile, in a satisfying hexagonal grid. The more you move, the more your world comes alive.

Your exploration, beautifully mapped
Hexplore uses a hexagonal grid system to track everywhere you've been. Each hex tile you reveal is permanently yours — a record of every street, trail, and path you've ever covered. Watch your personal map grow over time and discover just how much — or how little — of your local area you've actually explored.

Built for runners, walkers, and cyclists
Connect your Strava account to seed your map instantly with your full activity history. Import GPX files from any device. Or simply take your phone out for a run and watch the fog lift in real time as you move — like a Marauder's Map coming to life around you.

Explore smarter
Frontier highlighting shows you exactly where your explored territory ends — perfect for planning new routes into unmapped areas. Filter your map by transport mode to see what you've covered on foot versus by bike. Track your stats: total area explored, activities logged, personal bests, and more.

Your data stays yours
All your exploration data is stored privately on your device. Nothing is shared, sold, or uploaded. Hexplore simply helps you see the world you've already moved through — and inspires you to go further.

Free to explore
Get started for free and reveal your first 1,000 hex tiles. Upgrade to Hexplore Pro with a single one-time purchase for unlimited exploration, Strava import, achievements, and more.

Your world is out there. Go reveal it.
